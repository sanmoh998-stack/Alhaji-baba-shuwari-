About Alhaji Baba Shuwari

Alhaji Baba Shuwari is a businessman and community member from Ashaka, Funakaye Local Government Area, Gombe State, Nigeria.

He works with Ashaka Cement Company under the Production Operations department. Alongside his professional career, he has been involved in the maize business for about 20 years, gaining extensive experience in the buying and selling of maize.

He is also involved in real estate and property-related business. Within his community, Alhaji Baba Shuwari is known for his trustworthiness, helpfulness, and commitment to supporting people around him.
