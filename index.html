Game da Alhaji Baba Shuwari

English Profile

Alhaji Baba Shuwari is a businessman from Ashaka, Funakaye, Gombe State, Nigeria. He has been involved in maize trading for about 20 years and is also involved in real estate.

He is known for trustworthiness, good relationships with people, and supporting members of his community.
